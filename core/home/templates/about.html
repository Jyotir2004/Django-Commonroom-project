About Commonroom

A clearer view of your workspace.

Commonroom brings people, teams, and shared resources together in one place.

Good work depends on people being able to find one another and understand how they fit together. Commonroom gives your team a simple directory, a workspace overview, and room to keep useful information close at hand.

Registered accounts and invitations in the people directory are stored in the database. The vegetable catalog is sample data.
